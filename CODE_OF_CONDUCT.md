# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-focused community.

Participants must treat experiencers, witnesses, skeptics, researchers, operators, and contributors with dignity. Harassment, doxxing, threats, identity-based attacks, coercive disclosure, and knowingly misleading evidence presentation are not acceptable.

Disagreement about interpretation is expected. Critique claims, methods, and evidence without attacking people. Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security or maintainer contact mechanisms rather than publishing sensitive details.
