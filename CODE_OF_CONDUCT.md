# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-focused community.

Participants must treat contributors, users, reviewers, researchers, and operators with dignity. Harassment, doxxing, threats, identity-based attacks, coercive disclosure, and knowingly misleading evidence presentation are not acceptable.

Disagreement about interpretation is expected. Critique claims, methods, and evidence without attacking people. Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns through the repository's designated private reporting channels rather than publishing sensitive details.
