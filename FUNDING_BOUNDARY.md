# Funding Boundary

Frontier Decision Engine has an open public-interest surface and a potential institutional commercial layer. These must remain distinguishable.

## Open-source public layer

- source code
- schemas and methods
- sanitized examples
- public benchmark cases
- validation tools
- educational materials

## Bridge Node 7 commercial layer

May include, under separate terms:

- secure private deployments
- evidence vaults
- identity and access controls
- sensor and model integrations
- facilitated decision processes
- independent stress testing
- decision assurance
- training and support

## Charitable or donor-funded work

Charitable funding must not be represented as an unrestricted donation to Bridge Node 7. Any nonprofit or fiscally sponsored program requires separate governance, documented public benefit, conflict-of-interest controls, IP terms, procurement boundaries, and counsel review.

Donor-funded conclusions must remain intellectually independent. Funding does not confer authority to alter evidence classifications, suppress dissent, or predetermine findings.
