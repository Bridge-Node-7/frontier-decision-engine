# Governance

Frontier Decision Engine is governed as an open decision-method project. Open Phenomenon Verification is its flagship evidence profile, not an authority on the origin of any phenomenon.

## Principles

- Methods remain phenomenon-neutral.
- Raw evidence and generated media are never interchangeable.
- Human review remains visible and attributable.
- Uncertainty and disagreement are preserved.
- Privacy defaults are conservative.
- Public conclusions may not exceed the evidence level.

## Decision process

Routine changes use pull requests and review. Changes to evidence levels, schemas, privacy boundaries, or conclusion language require an RFC under `docs/rfcs/` and documented maintainer approval.

No sponsor, donor, contributor, model, or institution may purchase a case conclusion.
