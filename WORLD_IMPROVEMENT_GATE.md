# World Improvement Gate

Every feature, dataset, profile, integration, partnership, and release must answer these questions before approval.

1. **Decision quality:** Does it help someone make a more defensible decision?
2. **Evidence integrity:** Can conclusions be traced to sources, transformations, and assumptions?
3. **Human agency:** Does a human remain accountable for consequential judgment?
4. **Safety:** Could the capability increase surveillance, manipulation, discrimination, conflict, or physical harm?
5. **Privacy:** Can the benefit be achieved with less collection or exposure of sensitive information?
6. **Pluralism:** Can legitimate competing perspectives and dissent be represented?
7. **Accessibility:** Can affected nonexperts understand and challenge the important result?
8. **Reproducibility:** Can an independent reviewer verify the reasoning and calculations?
9. **Adaptability:** Does the system support learning and revision as conditions change?
10. **Net public benefit:** Is the expected public benefit greater than the expected harm, exclusion, and complexity?

A proposal that fails this gate must be redesigned, restricted, deferred, or rejected.

## Evidence required for a positive claim of impact

Do not claim that a release improves the world solely because it is open source or technically impressive. Capture evidence such as:

- fewer consequential errors
- clearer assumptions
- more inclusive deliberation
- faster recognition of plan vulnerabilities
- better reversibility or contingency planning
- reduced privacy exposure
- improved independent reproducibility
- documented user or institutional outcomes
